Our Story

Born in Slovenia,
crafted for the world

Zakistar began with a simple insight: walking barefoot on pebbles feels incredible. Our founder, walking the shores of Slovenian lakes, wondered why this natural therapy couldn't be part of everyday life.

Working with local artisans, we developed a method to set river stones into comfortable footwear. Each pair is a collaboration between nature and human hands — pebbles from Slovenian rivers, leather from local tanners, and straps woven with care.

We believe that true wellness is found in the simple things. A walk. A connection to nature. The feeling of earth beneath your feet.
