Our Story

Born in Slovenia,
crafted for the world

Zakistar began with a simple insight: walking barefoot on pebbles feels incredible. Strolling the shores of Slovenian lakes, our founder wondered why this natural therapy couldn't be part of everyday life.

Working with local artisans, we developed a method to set natural river stones into comfortable footwear — a collaboration between nature and human hands.

True wellness lives in the simple things. A walk. A connection to nature. The feeling of earth beneath your feet.

FAQ

Common questions

Are the pebbles comfortable to walk on?
Yes! The pebbles are smooth, rounded river stones carefully selected for comfort. They provide a gentle massaging sensation — like walking on a pebble beach, but without the sharp edges. Most customers find them comfortable from the first wear.
How do I clean my Zakistar flip flops?
Simply rinse with fresh water and mild soap if needed. Air dry away from direct sunlight. The natural materials are durable and easy to maintain.
Can I order a custom pair?
Absolutely. You can choose your strap color, pebble arrangement, and even send us your own stones. Custom orders start at €129. Just contact us through the form below.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship worldwide from Slovenia. Shipping times vary by destination. We use tracked shipping on all orders.
