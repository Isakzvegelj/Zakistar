Our Story

Born in Slovenia,
crafted for the world

Zakistar began with a simple insight: walking barefoot on pebbles feels incredible. Strolling the shores of Slovenian lakes, our founder wondered why this natural therapy couldn't be part of everyday life.

Working with local artisans, we developed a method to set natural river stones into comfortable footwear. Each pair is a collaboration between nature and human hands.

True wellness lives in the simple things. A walk. A connection to nature. The feeling of earth beneath your feet.
